01 · Heritage

1991, faithfully reinterpreted

GORILLA.BAS shipped inside MS-DOS 5 as a QBasic demo and became a generation's first physics lesson. We keep its rules verbatim and rebuild everything the rules sit inside.

03 · The physics overlay

Every miss, explained

The original taught by punishment. Bananarc shows its work: after each throw the trajectory stays ghosted with a debrief card, and the sentence “wind cost you 9 meters” is what makes players feel the model.

While aimingINTENT

Launch vector with live component decomposition and a short windless ghost arc — enough to communicate shape, never the landing point. No aim assist, ever.

During flightTRACE

Dotted trace behind the banana; a tangent velocity arrow shrinks vertically toward the apex and flips — projectile motion, performed.

After the throwDEBRIEF

The trajectory stays ghosted until the next player commits. The card compares actual landing vs. windless landing. Compare, adjust, throw again — the learning loop of the whole game.

Purist optionCLASSIC

Overlay verbosity: Full / Minimal / Off. Classic mode is 1991 with better fur.

Teal curtain-wall glass

Roughness 0.08, layered reflections from a night-sky HDRI; interior tungsten spill through blinds. The moon streaks across panels as the camera parallaxes.

Weathered brick

Scan-based albedo, roughness 0.85, normal-mapped mortar courses, decade of soot in the crevices. Crater edges expose scorched rebar and shattered floor slabs.

Concrete & rooftop grit

Matte slabs, water stains under every parapet; AC units, antennas with beacon lights, pigeon silhouettes — real rooftop staging for the gorillas.

Gorilla fur

Full groom on Mac/high-tier devices, baked shell cards elsewhere; anisotropic sheen, wind-reactive guard hairs, cool moonlit rim separating fur from sky.

Banana wax

Subsurface scattering ~0.3 mm of warmth, clearcoat highlight tracking the spin — the most-watched object in the game gets the most expensive shader.

Light itself

One cool moon key (~4,100 K), thousands of warm tungsten fills (~2,700 K), sodium haze at the horizon. Explosions are the only hot source — they momentarily flip the key. Earn them.

Camera & grade

Fixed side-on, near-orthographic long lens — 3D volumes with the classic silhouette. Shallow depth of field softens the distant skyline; a filmic tone curve, gentle vignette, and fine 35 mm grain finish the frame. The camera never cuts during flight: readability beats cinema in an aiming game.

Motion principles

Weight over snappiness: gorillas are heavy, and every throw earns its distance through anticipation. Physics objects (banana, debris) obey the simulation exactly; characters are hand-keyed against it. Nothing pops — everything follows through.

Character animation set (per gorilla)

Idleloop 4 s

Weight-shift sway, knuckle taps, occasional glance at the wind ribbon; fur and scarf ride the ambient breeze.

Windup → throw1.4 s

Full anticipation crouch, hip rotation, release synced to the simulation's launch frame. Velocity value scales the windup intensity.

Near-miss flinch0.6 s

Duck and cover when a banana lands within one building — comedy and feedback in one beat.

Defeat1.8 s

Ragdoll blend to a seated slump; dignity intact, rematch implied.

Victory dance3 loops

The sacred chest-beat, escalating each loop; haptic thumps on every strike. This is the trailer shot.
