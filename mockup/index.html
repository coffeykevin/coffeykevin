01 · Heritage

1991, faithfully reinterpreted

GORILLA.BAS shipped inside MS-DOS 5 as a QBasic demo and became a generation's first physics lesson. We keep its rules verbatim and rebuild everything the rules sit inside.

The charcoal control bar

Segmented POWER meter (yellow, 0–100) and ANGLE meter (cyan, 0–90) flank a circular FIRE button. Tap either readout to type exact numbers — the 1991 ritual, kept sacred. Power maps linearly onto classic velocity.

White dotted trace

The banana leaves a trail of clean white dots — the classic trace, restaged. It persists after the shot as the ghost the next player aims against.

Physics overlay, toggleable

Launch vector in player color with faint v·cosθ / v·sinθ components at the muzzle. Full / Minimal / Off — the concept frame shows Minimal; Classic mode is pure 1991.

Golden-hour staging

Low warm sun backlights the landmark spire and water tower; storm clouds break into amber haze. Long shadows and rim-lit fur are the scene's signature image.

Banana launchers

Gorillas sit at the parapet and shoulder-fire their launchers — brace, recoil, muzzle smoke. The launcher angle mirrors the ANGLE meter in real time.

Name tags & score pips

Floating tags with pointer triangles — yellow Kilo, cyan Newton, matching their meters — with round pips underneath. First to three.

03 · The physics overlay

Every miss, explained

The original taught by punishment. Bananarc shows its work: after each shot the dotted trace stays ghosted with a debrief card, and the sentence “wind cost you 9 meters” is what makes players feel the model.

While aimingINTENT

Launch vector at the muzzle with live component decomposition and a short windless ghost arc — enough to communicate shape, never the landing point. No aim assist, ever.

During flightTRACE

White dots behind the banana; a tangent velocity arrow shrinks vertically toward the apex and flips — projectile motion, performed.

After the shotDEBRIEF

The trace stays ghosted until the next player commits. The card compares actual landing vs. windless landing. Compare, adjust, fire again — the learning loop of the whole game.

Purist optionCLASSIC

Overlay verbosity: Full / Minimal / Off. Classic mode is 1991 with better fur.

WORLD: WARM & ATMOSPHERIC · HUD: CHARCOAL + YELLOW/CYAN · TRACE: PURE WHITE

Brownstone & brick

Scan-based masonry, roughness ~0.85, deep-set sash windows with painted frames; cornices and fire escapes catch the last warm light.

Backlit limestone

The landmark spire reads almost luminous — sun-facing faces near-blown, shadow faces hazed into the sky. Atmosphere does the modeling.

Water-tower timber

Weathered cedar staves, steel hoops, conical cap; the classic rooftop silhouette and a favorite thing to blow up.

Gorilla fur

Full strand groom on Mac/high tiers, shell cards elsewhere; golden rim light tracing the back and crown is the scene's signature.

Banana & launcher

Waxy subsurface banana with a clearcoat spin highlight; matte parkerized-steel launcher tube with worn edges and a leather strap.

Light itself

One low warm key (~3,000 K) raking across the city; storm-grey fill from the cloud deck; scattered tungsten windows in shadowed facades. Explosions punch holes in the calm.

Camera & grade

Fixed side-on, near-orthographic long lens — deep 3D staging (foreground brownstones, mid rooftops, hazed landmarks) with the classic silhouette. Warm filmic tone curve, gentle vignette, fine 35 mm grain. The camera never cuts during flight: readability beats cinema in an aiming game.

Motion principles

Weight over snappiness: the launcher has real recoil, the gorillas brace before firing, smoke hangs in the golden air. Physics objects obey the simulation exactly; characters are hand-keyed against it. Nothing pops — everything follows through.

Character animation set (per gorilla)

Idleloop 4 s

Seated weight-shift, launcher resting across the lap, occasional glance at the wind pill; fur rides the breeze.

Shoulder → fire1.4 s

Hoist, brace, tube tracks the ANGLE meter live, recoil kick + muzzle smoke synced to the simulation's launch frame. Power scales the recoil.

Near-miss flinch0.6 s

Duck behind the parapet when a banana lands within one building — comedy and feedback in one beat.

Defeat1.8 s

Ragdoll blend to a seated slump against the chimney; dignity intact, rematch implied.

Victory dance3 loops

Launcher raised overhead, then the sacred chest-beat, escalating each loop; haptic thumps on every strike. This is the trailer shot.

05 · Every Apple screen

One purchase, four rooms

Universal Purchase across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV. Same deterministic simulation everywhere — a shot is just a seed, an angle, and a power.

iOS · LANDSCAPE

iPhone

Thumb-reach meters + slingshot drag; haptic detents every 5° and 5 power. Pass-and-play hot-seat with a turn hand-off screen.

iPadOS

iPad

The couch showcase: split HUD, one player per bezel edge. Pencil hover previews the meters; pointer support throughout.

macOS · NATIVE

Mac

Keyboard-first, exactly like 1991: type the angle, Tab, type the power, Return to fire. Scroll wheel fine-tunes. Full groom fur tier.

tvOS · 10-FOOT

Apple TV

Siri Remote swipe for angle, click-pad edges for power; or any game controller. Oversized meters legible at three meters — the party build.
