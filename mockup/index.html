Launch vector, decomposed live

The solid arrow is the throw; dashed arrows are v·cosθ and v·sinθ with real numbers. Drag the dial or tap the readouts to type — the 1991 ritual, kept sacred.

Dotted trace & apex tick

The banana draws its trajectory as it flies. At the apex, vertical velocity hits zero and the overlay says so — the single clearest physics beat in the game.

Wind ribbon

Direction and strength, re-rolled each round. Wind drift is quadratic in flight time — slow lobs bend far more than fastballs, exactly as in the original.

Persistent craters

Misses carve volumetric chunks out of buildings, exposing glowing interior floors. The level remembers the whole duel.

Sol reacts

The sun goes shocked when overflown, squints at explosions, and naps if you take too long to aim. A silent third character.

Launch vector with live component decomposition and a short windless ghost arc — enough to communicate shape, never the landing point. No aim assist, ever.

During flightTRACE

Dotted trace behind the banana; a tangent velocity arrow shrinks vertically toward the apex and flips — projectile motion, performed.

After the throwDEBRIEF

The trajectory stays ghosted until the next player commits. The card compares actual landing vs. windless landing. Compare, adjust, throw again — the learning loop of the whole game.

Teal curtain-wall glass

Low-roughness panels with warm window bounce; reflections catch the moon, never the camera.

Weathered brick

High-roughness, subtle normal detail; crater edges expose scorched mortar and rebar.

Concrete & rooftop grit

Matte slabs with AC units, antennas, pigeon silhouettes — rooftop staging for the gorillas.

Gorilla fur

Groomed cards/shells, wind-reactive; warm rim light from the city separates fur from night sky.

Banana wax

Slight subsurface warmth, glossy highlight tracking the spin — the most-watched object in the game.

Light itself

One moon key light, thousands of warm window fills. Explosions are the only hot light source — earn them.

Camera & staging

Fixed side-on, near-orthographic long lens — 3D volumes with the classic silhouette. Background parallax only; a gentle push-in is reserved for the hit moment. The frame never cuts during flight: readability beats cinema in an aiming game.

Motion principles

Weight over snappiness: gorillas are heavy, and every throw earns its distance through anticipation. Physics objects (banana, debris) obey the simulation exactly; characters are hand-keyed against it. Nothing pops — everything follows through.

Character animation set (per gorilla)

Idleloop 4 s

Weight-shift sway, knuckle taps, occasional glance at the wind ribbon.

Windup → throw1.4 s

Full anticipation crouch, hip rotation, release synced to the simulation's launch frame. Velocity value scales the windup intensity.

Near-miss flinch0.6 s

Duck and cover when a banana lands within one building — comedy and feedback in one beat.

Defeat1.8 s

Ragdoll blend to a seated slump; dignity intact, rematch implied.

Victory dance3 loops

The sacred chest-beat, escalating each loop; haptic thumps on every strike. This is the trailer shot.
