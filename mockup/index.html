01 · Heritage

1991, faithfully reinterpreted

GORILLA.BAS shipped inside MS-DOS 5 as a QBasic demo and became a generation's first physics lesson. We keep its rules verbatim and rebuild everything the rules sit inside.

The full-width control bar

PLAYER 1 · POWER · WIND · ANGLE · PLAYER 2, per the concept. Names and score pips in player colors at the ends, segmented meters inboard, wind readout dead center. Tap any readout to type exact numbers — the 1991 ritual, kept sacred.

Bare-handed throws

No weapons — the gorillas wind up and hurl the banana with their whole body, exactly like the original. Kilo is caught at full follow-through; the throw fires from the gorilla (tap-and-hold, Return on Mac, A on controller), keeping the bar symmetric.

Weather & time label

Every round rolls a sky — "SUNSET · 7:45 PM" here. Condition and clock always read in the top-left so the variance feels like a feature, never a bug.

White dotted trace

The banana leaves a trail of clean white dots — the classic trace, restaged. It persists after the throw as the ghost the next player aims against.

Physics overlay, toggleable

Launch vector in player color with faint v·cosθ / v·sinθ components at the release point. Full / Minimal / Off — this frame shows Minimal; Classic mode is pure 1991.

Two numbers, always

Power 0–100 maps linearly onto the classic velocity range and Angle mirrors the original degrees — 1991 muscle memory transfers untouched, whatever the sky is doing.

03 · Weather & light

One skyline, nine skies

Every round rolls a weather condition and a clock time from the match seed — deterministic, so async opponents see the identical sky. Weather is presentation-first: the physics never change. The only coupling is an optional "Weather wind" setting that skews the classic wind roll to fit the sky, always within the original bounds.

04 · The physics overlay

Every miss, explained

The original taught by punishment. Bananarc shows its work: after each shot the dotted trace stays ghosted with a debrief card, and the sentence “wind cost you 9 meters” is what makes players feel the model.

While aimingINTENT

Launch vector at the release point with live component decomposition and a short windless ghost arc — enough to communicate shape, never the landing point. No aim assist, ever.

During flightTRACE

White dots behind the banana; a tangent velocity arrow shrinks vertically toward the apex and flips — projectile motion, performed.

After the throwDEBRIEF

The trace stays ghosted until the next player commits. The card compares actual landing vs. windless landing. Compare, adjust, throw again — the learning loop of the whole game.

Purist optionCLASSIC

Overlay verbosity: Full / Minimal / Off. Classic mode is 1991 with better fur.

Brownstone & brick

Scan-based masonry, roughness ~0.85, deep-set sash windows with painted frames; cornices and fire escapes catch the last warm light.

Backlit limestone

The landmark spire reads almost luminous — sun-facing faces near-blown, shadow faces hazed into the sky. Atmosphere does the modeling.

Water-tower timber

Weathered cedar staves, steel hoops, conical cap; the classic rooftop silhouette and a favorite thing to blow up.

Gorilla fur

Full strand groom on Mac/high tiers, shell cards elsewhere; golden rim light tracing the back and crown is the scene's signature.

Banana wax

Waxy subsurface warmth with a clearcoat highlight tracking the spin — the most-watched object in the game gets the most expensive shader, in every weather.

Light itself

One physically-driven sky rig, nine states: sun position from the round's clock time, cloud deck density from the condition, tungsten windows rising as light falls. Explosions punch holes in whatever calm the sky chose.

Camera & grade

Fixed side-on, near-orthographic long lens — deep 3D staging (foreground brownstones, mid rooftops, hazed landmarks) with the classic silhouette. Warm filmic tone curve, gentle vignette, fine 35 mm grain. The camera never cuts during flight: readability beats cinema in an aiming game.

Motion principles

Weight over snappiness: the gorillas coil and uncork the whole body into every throw, and weather answers — fur mats in rain, breath fogs in snow rounds. Physics objects obey the simulation exactly; characters are hand-keyed against it. Nothing pops — everything follows through.

Character animation set (per gorilla)

Idleloop 4 s

Weight-shift sway, knuckle taps on the parapet, occasional glance at the wind readout; fur rides whatever the weather sends.

Windup → throw1.4 s

Full-body coil — hips, shoulder, arm — release synced to the simulation's launch frame, held follow-through. Power scales the windup intensity; the arm angle tracks the ANGLE meter live.

Near-miss flinch0.6 s

Duck behind the parapet when a banana lands within one building — comedy and feedback in one beat.

Defeat1.8 s

Ragdoll blend to a seated slump against the chimney; dignity intact, rematch implied.

Victory dance3 loops

Arms overhead, then the sacred chest-beat, escalating each loop; haptic thumps on every strike. This is the trailer shot.
