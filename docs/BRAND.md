# Naming & Brand

Companion to `docs/PRD.md`. Visual boards live in `mockup/index.html`.

## Why not "Gorillas"

The original name is unavailable in practice: an active European delivery
brand, general crowding of "gorilla" marks in games, and Nintendo adjacency
(Donkey Kong) make it a fight not worth having. The nostalgia hook survives
in marketing copy ("inspired by the classic 1991 QBasic artillery duel")
without the name being on the box.

## Name shortlist

| # | Name | Rationale | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bananarc** ★ | Banana + arc — names the projectile *and* the physics in one invented, ownable word. Reads clean in every target locale, App Store searchable, .com viable. | **Recommended.** |
| 2 | **Rooftop Rumble** | The setting + the duel. Warm, couch-game energy. | Alliteration is charming but crowded; weaker physics signal. |
| 3 | **Going Ballistic** | Literal ballistics pun; great trailer line. | Better tagline than title; idiom may not localize. |
| 4 | **Ape Arc** | Minimal, geometric, looks great as a wordmark. | Short marks are hardest to clear; "APEX" collisions. |
| 5 | **Kilo & Newton** | Character-led (our two gorillas); physics units as names. | Lovely for a sequel/show; asks marketing to explain the game. |
| 6 | **Skyline Slam** | Action + setting. | Sports-game vibes; wrong shelf. |
| 7 | **Banana Ballistics** | Fully literal. | Long; safe; a fine fallback if Bananarc fails clearance. |
| 8 | **Hurl!** | One verb, high energy. | UK slang problem; hard to clear. |

## The brand: BANANARC

- **Tagline:** *Angle. Velocity. Bananas.*
  (Alt: "The physics of settling arguments." / "Old-school artillery,
  rebuilt.")
- **Logo:** heavy geometric sans wordmark, tight-tracked; the second **A**'s
  crossbar is replaced by a small banana mid-arc with a dotted trajectory
  entering from the first syllable — the mark literally throws.
- **App icon:** DOS-blue field, single banana at 45° with a dotted arc and a
  crescent of teal skyline in the lower corner. No gorilla on the icon —
  the banana is the brand.
- **Color (EGA-derived, modernized):**
  - `#1424C8` DOS Blue — sky, brand field
  - `#FFD23F` Banana — primary accent, CTAs
  - `#17B9B4` Teal — Kilo / player 1, building glass
  - `#C6392F` Brick — Newton / player 2, building brick
  - `#FFE9A8` Window Glow — warm lights, highlights
  - `#060B2E` Midnight — ground/ink
- **Voice:** deadpan sports-commentary warmth. Short sentences. Physics
  terms used correctly and casually ("Apex: 41 m. Wind ate 9 of them.").
  Never zany, never corporate.
- **Characters:** **Kilo** (teal scarf, left rooftop, showboat) and
  **Newton** (red scarf, right rooftop, methodical). The sun is **Sol** —
  a silent third character who reacts to everything.
- **Store positioning:** "A premium remake-in-spirit of the 1991 classic.
  One price. No ads. Couch duels on every Apple screen."
